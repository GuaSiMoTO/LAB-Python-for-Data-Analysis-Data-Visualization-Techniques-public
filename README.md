# LAB: Python for Data Analysis - Data Visualization Techniques

## Introduction

Welcome to the Data Visualization Techniques lab. This session builds upon your foundational knowledge of pandas by introducing you to powerful visualization libraries like Matplotlib and Seaborn. Data visualization is not just about making pretty graphics—it's about telling stories and making insights from the data visible and actionable. This lab will equip you with the skills to create compelling visual representations of data sets.

## Initial Setup

### Fork and Clone the Repository
Start by forking the provided GitHub repository to your account and then clone it locally. This will be your workspace for completing the lab exercises.

## Scenario

You are now working as a junior data analyst. Your manager wants to understand the sales trends for different products throughout the year to aid in upcoming marketing strategies. You've been tasked to visualize this data to identify seasonal trends, product popularity, and potential areas for sales campaigns.

## Iteration 1: Getting Started with Matplotlib

Set up your Python environment ensuring both pandas and Matplotlib are installed.

- Load your dataset (focus on sales data).
- Use Matplotlib to plot the first simple bar chart showing sales per month.
- Customize your plot with labels and a title to improve readability.

## Iteration 2: Advanced Plotting with Seaborn

Now that you are comfortable with basic plots, let's move to more complex visualizations using Seaborn.

- Generate a heatmap to visualize sales data correlations or time-based aggregations.
- Create a pair plot to explore relationships between continuous variables in the dataset.

## Iteration 3: Customizing Plots

Effective visualization requires customization to highlight key insights.

- Enhance your plots from previous iterations by adding annotations, using color maps, and adjusting axes properties for clarity and visual appeal.
- Experiment with Seaborn’s styles and color palettes to prepare presentation-ready figures.

## Iteration 4: Real-world Practice

Apply your skills to a real-world dataset:

- You will be provided with a dataset from a retail company. Your task is to generate a series of visualizations to answer specific business questions like seasonal effects on sales, the performance of different categories of products, and the impact of promotions.
- Summarize your findings with visual aids and present them as if to a stakeholder.

## Bonus Challenge

For those looking to push further:

- Integrate interactive elements into your visualizations using Plotly or Bokeh.
- Explore and implement subplots to create a dashboard-like layout of multiple plots.

## How to Submit

Once you have completed your analysis and visualizations, push your Jupyter notebook to your forked GitHub repository. Ensure that your final submission includes narratives explaining your visualizations and insights.
